Account deletion

Delete your account

You can delete your Amble Couples account and the information that goes with it at any time, from the app or by email. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

In the app

Profile → Delete account. It's instant. See the steps

Without the app

Email us and we'll delete it for you within 30 days. How to ask

1Delete in the app

  1. Open Amble Couples and sign in.
  2. On the Home tab, tap your photos at the top to open Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. If you like, tell us why you're leaving. This is optional.
  5. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out on that phone.

2Ask us by email

Don't have the app any more, or can't sign in? Email us from the address you use for your Amble Couples account, with the subject "Delete my account".

alicheema12455@gmail.com

We may ask you to confirm the account is yours before we delete it. We'll finish within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

3What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, email address and profile, including your name, nickname, birthday and profile photo.
  • Your connection to your partner.
  • Everything you added: moments, photos, voice notes, doodles, letters, songs, dreams, book pages and answers to daily questions.
  • Your notification tokens, so we can't send notifications to your phones any more.
  • Your purchase records with us and with RevenueCat, our subscription provider.

4What is kept, and for how long

InformationWhat happens
Things your partner addedThey stay with your partner, because they belong to them. Chapters you created stay in their book without your name.
Your reason for leavingIf you choose one, it's kept without your name, email or account ID.
BackupsDeleted information can stay in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it's overwritten.
Technical logsKept for a short time, usually no more than 30 days, then deleted.
Store purchase recordsApple and Google keep their own records of your purchases under their own privacy policies.

Paying for Premium? Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play subscriptions or in your App Store subscriptions so you aren't charged again.

5Delete some data and keep your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove things:

  • Delete any moment, photo, doodle, dream, book page or song you added, right where it appears in the app.
  • Remove your profile photo in Profile → edit profile.
  • Unpair in Profile. Your shared space is kept for 30 days in case you pair again, then it's permanently deleted with all of its photos, voice notes and doodles.

For anything else, email alicheema12455@gmail.com and we'll help.
