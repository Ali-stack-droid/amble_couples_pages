1Who we are

2Information we collect

We only collect what Amble needs to work. Here is all of it.

2.1 Your account

  • Your email address and password if you sign up with email. Passwords are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, so we can't read them.
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and an account ID from them. Apple lets you hide your email, in which case we only get a private relay address.
  • One-time codes we email you to confirm your address or reset your password.
  • An account ID that we create for you.

2.2 Your profile

  • Your first and last name, your nickname and the nickname you use for your partner.
  • Your birthday and love languages, if you add them.
  • Your gender and age range, if you choose to answer when you start. Your partner can't see them.
  • Your profile photo, if you add one.
  • The date you got together and your answers to the "How we began" questions, if you answer them.
  • Your time zone, so reminders and notifications arrive at the right time for you.

The cheat sheet has an optional Allergies field. If you fill it in, that may count as health information. We only use it to show it to your partner, and you can clear it any time.

2.4 Things you choose to share with your partner

Each of these has its own switch in the app, and nothing is shared until you turn it on.

  • Local time: your time zone, so your partner can see what time it is for you.
  • Weather: the city you type in. To get the weather, the app sends only that city name to Open-Meteo. We don't use GPS and never ask for location access.
  • Battery: your phone's battery level and whether it's charging.

Heartbeat touch makes your partner's phone pulse while you hold the heart. We only send the fact that you're holding it. We don't measure your heart rate or any other health data.

Spicy questions: we store whether your Spicy questions switch is on. Your partner can see it, because these spicy romantic questions only appear when you both turn it on.

2.5 Your device and notifications

  • A push notification token from Apple or Google, plus your phone's platform, app version and language, so notifications reach the right phone.
  • Your notification settings and quiet hours.
  • A scrambled key for each home-screen widget, so it can show your latest doodle.

2.6 Purchases

If you buy Amble Premium, Apple or Google handles the payment. We and RevenueCat (our subscription provider) receive your plan, its status, its start, renewal and end dates, and the store's transaction IDs. We never see your card or bank details.

2.7 When you contact us

Your email address and whatever you choose to tell us.

2.8 When you delete your account

You can tell us why you're leaving. That reason is saved without your name, email or account ID.

2.9 Technical logs

Like most online services, our servers record basic technical details when the app talks to them, such as your IP address, the time, your account ID and any errors. We use them to keep Amble secure and to fix problems.

What we don't collect: your precise location, your contacts, your browsing history or advertising IDs. Amble has no ads, no analytics and no crash-tracking tools.

3How we use your information

  • To run Amble: create your account, sign you in, pair you with your partner, store and sync what you add, and show it on both phones and your widgets.
  • To send the notifications you allow, like "Sam sent you a doodle", while respecting your quiet hours.
  • To understand who uses Amble: we look at gender and age ranges only as totals, to make Amble better for the people who use it.
  • To provide Premium: check whether your couple has Premium and apply the free plan's limits.
  • To keep Amble safe: prevent abuse (for example, limiting how often pairing codes can be tried), protect accounts and fix bugs.
  • To talk to you: send sign-in codes, answer your emails and tell you about important changes. We don't send marketing emails.
  • To follow the law when it requires us to keep or share information.

We don't use your memories, photos or messages to build advertising profiles or to train AI models, and we don't use them for anything other than running Amble for the two of you.

4Legal bases

If you live in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: most of what we do is needed to give you the app you signed up for.
  • Consent: for optional things, such as notifications, sharing your time, weather and battery, your profile photo, and your gender and age range. For the Allergies field, filling it in is your explicit consent. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping Amble secure, preventing abuse, fixing bugs, and learning from the reasons people give when they leave. We always weigh these against your rights.
  • Legal obligation: when the law requires us to keep or share information.

Please only add things you're happy for your partner to see. Like any message, they can save or screenshot what you share, and we can't control that.

6Unpairing and your shared space

  • Either of you can unpair from Profile at any time. It ends the connection for both of you straight away, and neither of you can see the shared book, doodles, letters, songs or dreams any more.
  • We keep the shared space for 30 days in case you change your mind. If the two of you pair again within that time, everything comes back.
  • After 30 days, the shared space and all of its photos, voice notes and doodles are permanently deleted.
  • Your own things stay with your account: your profile, mood, cheat sheet, sharing switches, idea swipes, "How we began" answers and settings.
  • If your partner was paying for Premium, it ends for you when you unpair.

8Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time:

  1. Open Amble and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. It happens straight away.

What's deleted: your sign-in, profile and profile photo, your connection to your partner, the moments, photos, voice notes, doodles, letters, songs, dreams and answers you added, your notification tokens, and your purchase records with us and RevenueCat.

What stays: things your partner added stay with them. Chapters you created stay in their book without your name.

Paying for Premium? Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Please cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Don't have the app any more? Email support@amblecouples.app from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm it's you, and we'll finish within 30 days.

10How we protect your information

  • Everything is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Rules on our servers make sure only you and your partner can read your shared space. They're enforced on the server, not just in the app.
  • Photos, voice notes and profile photos are kept in private storage. The app opens them through links that expire after 7 days.
  • Pairing code attempts are limited to stop anyone guessing codes.
  • A copy of your data is kept on your phone so Amble works offline. Signing out clears it from the app, so please protect your phone with a passcode.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your information, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
